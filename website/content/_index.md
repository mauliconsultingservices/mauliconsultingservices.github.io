Mauli Consulting Services is an **India-based engineering practice** — **one principal-level, hands-on engineer** embedded with your team, not a bench of juniors. Work is **end-to-end**: design, implementation, automated testing, deployment, and production support. We accept a **limited number of full-time retainers** with teams that need **senior IC / staff-equivalent** depth; **commercial terms are agreed privately** after a short discovery call. Engagements are **B2B services contracts** (not employment), delivered **remote from India**.

## What we build

- **Distributed systems & APIs** — microservices, REST, gRPC, GraphQL; event-driven and 12-factor style services  
- **Polyglot backends** — Java (Spring), Go, Node.js/TypeScript, Python; production code, reviews, and hardening  
- **Full-stack product delivery** — React/TypeScript front ends when the product needs them; not a “backend-only” shop  
- **Agentic AI & agents** — **LangChain** / **LangGraph**: multi-step agents, tool calling, orchestration, and production integration with your APIs (not research-only ML)  
- **RAG & knowledge** — retrieval pipelines, embeddings, document ingestion, and search-backed answers wired into product workflows  
- **MCP server development** — custom **Model Context Protocol** servers and tool surfaces so assistants connect safely to your systems, repos, and internal APIs  
- **Cloud-native platforms** — AWS, Azure, GCP; containers (Docker); **Kubernetes** (EKS and kin), operators, GitOps, service mesh where the product requires it  
- **Data & search** — PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Elasticsearch/OpenSearch; **Apache Spark / EMR** and pipeline work when the domain needs it  
- **Engineering discipline** — CI/CD (GitLab, GitHub Actions, Jenkins), observability (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK), integration and contract testing
